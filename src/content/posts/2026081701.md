A paper template should disappear when the reader starts reading—but it should still make the first page feel intentional. **Kiramei Preprint Style** is my attempt to balance those two goals: a recognizable visual identity on the surface and a practical, reusable academic workflow underneath.

## One manuscript, two layouts

The same `main.tex` can produce a spacious single-column preprint or a compact two-column manuscript. Switching is a document-class option rather than a rewrite:

```tex
% Single column
\documentclass[11pt,letterpaper]{template/style}

% Two columns
\documentclass[11pt,letterpaper,twocolumn]{template/style}
```

That small decision keeps figures, tables, references, and mathematical notation in one source tree while allowing the document to adapt to different reading and submission contexts.

## More than a decorated title page

The template coordinates Linux Libertine text, NewTX mathematics, and Inconsolata monospaced type. It includes a compact title and author system, running titles, keywords, configurable footer metadata, theorem environments, and reusable mathematical commands.

For full manuscripts, the surrounding toolchain matters just as much as typography. The repository includes ready-to-use support for:

- Natbib and BibTeX references;
- booktabs tables and subfigures;
- algorithms and source-code listings;
- TikZ and PGFPlots figures;
- numbered or unnumbered sections;
- optional copyright, internal-document, address, and logo modes.

`main.tex` is both a compilable example and a usage guide, so common elements can be copied from a working document instead of reconstructed from package documentation.

## Build and customize

The tested build path uses XeLaTeX through `latexmk`:

```bash
latexmk -xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
```

Most paper content stays in `main.tex` and `main.bib`. Package configuration lives in `template/preamble.tex`, reusable notation in `template/command.tex`, and document-wide appearance in `template/style.cls`. This separation keeps everyday writing simple without hiding the deeper controls.
