Fast diffusion inference is often described as a compute problem, but cache acceleration can quietly become a memory problem. Saving features from many layers or modules avoids repeated work only by building a large activation buffer. **BaryCache** takes a leaner route: remember a short history of complete stepwise outputs and forecast the next feature with a stable barycentric extrapolator.

## What is kept—and what is deliberately not kept

In the recommended **stepwise** mode, BaryCache stores only a small number of full-step features. The default paper setting uses two anchors. It does not need to retain a separate activation history for every transformer block, which keeps the method compact as models and resolutions grow.

Its extrapolation weights are:

- computed from the schedule independently of feature values;
- clipped to suppress unstable extremes;
- decay-adjusted to reduce Runge-like oscillation;
- blended with the newest cached feature when a conservative prediction is preferable.

The model remains untouched: no finetuning, adapter, or distillation stage is required.

## Images and video in one framework

The repository covers three representative workloads:

- **DiT-XL/2** for 256×256 class-to-image generation;
- **PixArt-Sigma** for 512×512 text-to-image generation;
- **HunyuanVideo** for 480×640 text-to-video generation with 129-frame samples.

Each model directory is self-contained. The key controls—`fresh_interval`, `max_history`, `blend_factor`, scheduling mode, and stepwise versus blockwise caching—remain visible in the sample scripts, making the performance/quality decision inspectable rather than implicit.

## Main reported trade-offs

| Workload | Cache setting | Speedup | Peak VRAM | Quality snapshot |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| PixArt-Sigma T2I | N=6, H=2 | 3.30× | 3.941 GB | ImageReward 0.6654, CLIPScore 31.10 |
| HunyuanVideo T2V | N=7, H=2 | 3.017× | 30.805 GB | PSNR 17.9988, SSIM 0.6496 |
| DiT-XL/2 C2I | N=6, H=2 | 2.18× | 5.529 GB | FID 3.91, IS 209.77 |
