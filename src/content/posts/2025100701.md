Three covers, released one month apart, became a tiny musical diary. All three are songs by **Fuwari-P**, sung here by **Nayuka (那由歌)**. They share a gentle sense of motion: never rushing to the next feeling, leaving enough air around each line for the melody to glow.

## またあした — see you tomorrow

![またあした cover](/assets/posts/nayuka-mata-ashita.webp)

Published on **October 7, 2025**, this was the first of the three. The title means “see you tomorrow,” and the song carries the soft weight of a farewell that is not quite an ending. The long 5:32 arrangement gives the vocal room to move from restraint into warmth.

[Watch またあした on Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV16txDz4E5g/)

## ねがいごと — a wish held quietly

![ねがいごと cover](/assets/posts/nayuka-negai-goto.webp)

Released the next day, **October 8, 2025**, *ねがいごと* feels closer and more inward. Its melody unfolds like a wish that is easier to sing than to say aloud. The cover keeps that intimacy intact instead of forcing a dramatic peak.

[Watch ねがいごと on Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1YVxiz6EET/)

## きらり — the bright final page

![きらり cover](/assets/posts/nayuka-kirari.webp)

The set concluded on **November 8, 2025** with *きらり*. Compared with the two October songs, it has a clearer sparkle in both title and atmosphere. It feels like turning back toward the light after lingering for a while in memory.

[Watch きらり on Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Jc1DB9E74/)

Together, the three videos make more sense as a sequence than as isolated uploads: goodbye, wish, and glimmer. If you start with *またあした* and listen in release order, the month between the second and third cover feels almost like a deliberate pause.
