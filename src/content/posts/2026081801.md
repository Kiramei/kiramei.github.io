Diffusion Transformers create remarkable images by repeatedly running a large model across the denoising timeline. The quality is impressive; the repetition is expensive. **ChebBooster** asks a practical question: if nearby diffusion features move along a structured trajectory, can some of those full model evaluations be forecast instead?

The answer is a **training-free feature extrapolation framework** inspired by Chebyshev polynomial theory.

## From polynomial intuition to a stable cache

Naive high-order extrapolation can oscillate badly, especially when its nodes are poorly placed. ChebBooster uses equispaced normalized cache nodes together with a Chebyshev-inspired **barycentric formulation**. This makes the extrapolator numerically better behaved while retaining a lightweight online step.

The method splits its work into two phases:

1. **Offline:** precompute weights that depend on the diffusion schedule and cache pattern, not on model features.
2. **Online:** combine a short feature history with one weight multiplication whenever an extrapolated step replaces a full forward pass.

Because the model weights never change, the same idea can be applied without finetuning each backbone.

## Three model families, three scales

The release contains separate implementations for:

- **DiT-XL/2** at 256×256 class-to-image generation;
- **PixArt-Sigma** at 512×512 text-to-image generation;
- **FLUX.1-dev** at 1024×1024 text-to-image generation.

They remain in separate subtrees because their upstream environments, checkpoints, and licenses differ. Each implementation documents its own CUDA and dependency requirements rather than pretending that one combined environment is reliable.

## Reported results

The published configurations show the quality–speed trade-off at three very different workloads:

| Model | Quality snapshot | Latency speedup | FLOPs reduction |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| DiT-XL/2 | FID 2.25 | 1.674× | 2.771× |
| PixArt-Sigma | CLIP 33.1699, ImageReward 1.1222 | 2.018× | 3.652× |
| FLUX.1-dev | CLIP 31.61, ImageReward 0.9962 | 3.679× | 4.993× |

The repository also includes deliberately small smoke-test recipes before the paper-scale runs. That distinction matters when one full evaluation can mean hundreds or tens of thousands of generated images.
